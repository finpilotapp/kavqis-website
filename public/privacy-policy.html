1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how KAVQIS (“KAVQIS”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use the KAVQIS — Financial Intelligence mobile application (the “App”) and this website (the “Website”), together the “Services”.

We designed KAVQIS to help you understand everyday financial decisions, and we aim to collect as little personal information as possible to do that. By using the Services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you choose to give us

If you email us or use the contact form on this Website, we receive the information you provide, such as your name, email address, the topic you select and the content of your message.

Figures you enter into the App

To produce results, the App uses the numbers you enter into its calculators, such as loan amounts, interest rates, salary figures, savings goals or daily spending. KAVQIS does not ask for your bank account numbers, card details or online banking login credentials, and you should not enter them into the App.

Technical information

  • Website: like most websites, our hosting provider automatically processes standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit and the time of your visit, to deliver and secure the Website.
  • App: when you download the App through Google Play, Google may provide us with aggregated, non-identifying information such as install counts, device types and crash reports, in line with your Google account and device settings.

3. How we use information

We use the limited information described above to:

  • provide the App’s calculations and display your results;
  • respond to your questions, feedback and requests;
  • maintain, secure and improve the App and the Website, including fixing errors;
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the figures you enter to make financial product recommendations.

4. Storage on your device

Where the App saves your preferences, recent calculations or daily entries, this information is intended to be stored locally on your device so you can come back to it. You can remove this information at any time by clearing the App’s data in your device settings or uninstalling the App.

5. Sharing and service providers

We share information only where needed to operate the Services, with providers who process it on our behalf:

  • Netlify, which hosts this Website and processes contact form submissions.
  • Google, which distributes the App through Google Play and supplies the web fonts used on this Website. When a page loads, your browser requests these fonts from Google’s servers.
  • Email providers that deliver and store the messages you send to us.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition. Some providers may process information outside your country; where this happens, we rely on their safeguards for international transfers.

6. Data retention

We keep messages and contact form submissions only for as long as needed to respond to you and to keep a reasonable record of our correspondence, after which we delete them. Information stored locally by the App remains on your device until you delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we handle, including serving this Website over an encrypted HTTPS connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s privacy

The Services are not directed at children under the age of 13, or the minimum age required in your country, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email us at hellokavqis@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the App is released or new features are added. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be highlighted on this Website or in the App.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle information, please contact us:
