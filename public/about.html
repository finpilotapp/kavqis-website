About KAVQIS

Making financial calculations easier.

We’re building a toolkit that helps people understand the numbers behind everyday financial decisions: clearly, calmly and without jargon.

Our story

Why we’re building KAVQIS

Our vision is simple: anyone should be able to understand the numbers behind a money decision, without needing to be an expert.

Most financial decisions come down to a calculation. How much will this loan cost in total? How much do I need to save each month? What will my salary actually look like after tax? The maths usually isn’t difficult, but it’s scattered across spreadsheets, websites and fine print, and it rarely comes with an explanation.

KAVQIS brings those calculations together in one focused mobile app. Each module tackles one kind of decision, uses inputs you already know, and returns results in plain language, so you can understand the trade-offs before you commit.

We are not a bank, a lender or an adviser, and we don’t sell financial products. Our only goal is to make the numbers easier to understand, so the decisions you make are genuinely your own.

What guides us

Four commitments behind every screen.

Clarity

Clean screens, plain words and only the inputs that matter. If something can be simpler, we make it simpler.

Transparency

We show how results are worked out, so you can follow the logic rather than take a number on trust.

Honesty

Every calculation is an estimate based on your inputs. We label it that way and never overstate certainty.

Independence

No product recommendations and no hidden agenda. KAVQIS exists to inform your thinking, not steer it.

Setting expectations

What KAVQIS is, and what it isn’t.

Being clear about our role matters as much as being clear about the numbers.

KAVQIS is

  • A toolkit for exploring everyday financial calculations
  • A way to compare scenarios before you make a decision
  • A plain-language explanation of how results are reached
  • A good starting point for better conversations with professionals

KAVQIS isn’t

  • A bank, lender, broker or investment platform
  • A source of professional financial, investment, tax or legal advice
  • A guarantee of any rate, return or outcome
  • A replacement for advice tailored to your circumstances
