Why KAVQIS

Financial decisions shouldn’t need a spreadsheet, a finance degree and a free afternoon.

Most of us make important money decisions with half the picture — a monthly payment without the total cost, a return without the effect of time, a salary without the deductions. KAVQIS brings the calculations behind those decisions into one calm, focused app, so you can see the whole picture before you commit.

Six modules, one toolkit

Everything you need to think it through.

From the true cost of a home loan to what you can comfortably spend today, each module answers one specific question in plain language.

How it works

Three steps from question to clarity.

  1. STEP 01

    Pick the question

    Choose a module — a loan, an investment, your salary, a savings goal, or a quick everyday calculation.

  2. STEP 02

    Enter your numbers

    Use your own figures. Every input is labelled in plain language, with no jargon to decode.

  3. STEP 03

    See the whole picture

    Get a clear breakdown of totals, timelines and trade-offs, then adjust any input to see how the outcome changes.

Our principles

Built on clarity, not complexity.

KAVQIS is designed to help you understand, not to sell, push or persuade.

Read about our vision
  1. i.

    Plain language first

    Every result is explained in everyday words. If a term needs a definition, it gets one.

  2. ii.

    Show the working

    You see how a number was reached, not just the number itself, so you can follow it and question it.

  3. iii.

    Honest about estimates

    Results are estimates based on what you enter. We say so clearly, every time.

  4. iv.

    Your decision, your call

    KAVQIS informs your thinking. It doesn’t recommend financial products or tell you what to do.
