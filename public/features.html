Features

Six modules. One clear view of your money.

Each KAVQIS module focuses on one kind of decision, with inputs you understand and results explained in plain language.

Loans Investments Salary & Tax Savings Smart Tools Daily Money Dashboard
MODULE 01

Loans

Know the full cost before you borrow. See beyond the monthly payment to the total interest and how your repayments are split over time.

  • Monthly payment (EMI) estimates for home, car, personal and education loans
  • Total interest and total amount repayable over the full term
  • A year-by-year view of how principal and interest change
  • See how a different rate or term changes the outcome

MODULE 02

Investments

See how time and consistency compound. Understand the difference between what you put in and what it could grow into.

  • Growth estimates for regular monthly investments and one-time lump sums
  • A clear split between your contributions and estimated growth
  • Adjust the expected return, duration and contribution to compare scenarios
  • See how inflation affects what a future amount is really worth

MODULE 03

Salary & Tax

Understand what actually lands in your account. Break a gross salary down into tax, contributions and take-home pay.

  • Estimate take-home pay from your gross salary
  • A clear breakdown of estimated tax and common deductions
  • Switch between monthly and annual views
  • See the effect of a raise or a change in deductions

Tax rules vary by country and change over time. Results are estimates based on the inputs and assumptions you provide.

MODULE 04

Savings

Turn a goal into a plan. Work out how much to set aside, and how long it will take to get where you want to be.

  • Plan for any goal with a target amount and a target date
  • Find the monthly amount needed to reach it
  • Set an emergency fund target based on your expenses
  • See how interest on savings can shorten the timeline
